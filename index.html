FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?
Nope. Nimbus is built for non-developers. Everything you do in the visual builder — styling, layout, forms, publishing — requires zero code. Developers can still export clean HTML/CSS if they want to take over.
Can I connect my own domain?
Yes. On Pro and Business plans you can attach custom domains with automatic SSL certificates, redirects, and region routing through our global edge network.
What can I build with Nimbus?
Marketing sites, landing pages, internal dashboards, client portals, waitlists, admin panels, portfolio sites, and much more. If it renders in a browser, you can build it here.
Can my team work together?
Absolutely. Business workspaces include shared libraries, role-based permissions, real-time multi-user editing, and a full audit trail of every change.
What happens when I publish?
Your app is built into a production bundle, optimized, and deployed to our global edge in under a minute. You get a nimbus.app subdomain instantly, plus optional custom domains.
